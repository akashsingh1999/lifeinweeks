FAQ

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for "Life in Weeks", choose a size and tap Add Widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a widget.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I change my birthday?

Open Settings (gear icon) → Your life.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backup. It is never sent to us.

How do I turn off sounds?

Open Settings → Feel and turn off Sounds or Haptics. Sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch.
