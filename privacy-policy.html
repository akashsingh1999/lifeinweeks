Privacy Policy

Life in Weeks · Last updated November 23, 2026

Life in Weeks does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your data stays on your device

Your birthday, life expectancy, memories, goals and settings are saved only on your iPhone, so the app and its widgets can display them. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app works fully offline and has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Start over in the app, or delete the app, to remove all of your data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
